How the planner works

New topics become due the day after learning. After a review, the next review is 1, 2, 4, 7 or 14 days later depending on confidence. Due topics are scheduled using your preferred session length, daily limit and breaks. A coursework slot is added when there is room. No topic is invented. Manual sessions can use any logged topic and their own duration, start time and order. Completed sessions are retained when changing modes.
